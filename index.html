About

Numbers with a human point of view

I am an accounting student finishing my BS at Utah Valley University in December 2026, with a Master of Accountancy and the CPA path ahead. I am also a mom, which keeps me practical, organized and very good at focusing on what matters most.

My work so far has taken me from running the daily books of a retail store to analyzing NGO performance in Accra, Ghana and presenting findings in Tokyo. The common thread is the same: turning messy data into something people can trust and act on.

I believe most people and businesses can do far more than they think. Good financial clarity is often the first thing that makes the next bold step feel possible.

Anything is possible, and the numbers can show you the path.
